# Solar System Simulation

A **3D simulation of the Solar System**, featuring dynamic lighting, orbiting planets, and unique music for each celestial body.

## 🌌 Features
- **Sun**: Center of the system with dynamic lighting
- **Planets**: Mercury, Venus, Earth, and Mars orbit realistically
- **Moon**: Earth's natural satellite included
- **Zoom & Exploration**: Use `V` and `B` keys to zoom in/out and trigger planet-specific music
- **Music**: Each celestial body has its own unique soundtrack

## 🎮 Controls
- **Zoom In/Out**: `V` / `B`
- **Explore**: Navigate freely to view planets and the Sun

## 🎨 Visuals & Audio
- Dynamic lighting effects around the Sun
- Atmospheric music tied to each planet
- Simple but immersive orbital mechanics
